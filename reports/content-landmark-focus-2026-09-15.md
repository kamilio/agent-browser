## Implemented

`main-content-v1` is an explicit core, native-command and research CLI option.
It selects a unique nonempty outermost main, otherwise a unique nonempty outermost
article; ambiguity or absence falls back to the document. Selection reason,
candidate counts and scanned nodes accompany the actual scope. Existing native
roles, visibility/omission rules and caps apply. Scan failure is not a partial
choice. Manual scopes/discovery conflict, defaults remain unchanged, and replay
does not silently inherit focus. See `EXTRACTION-FOCUS.md` for the contract.

## Source-quality caution

Independent local review read all4 smaller scoped samples and compared full or
sampled document output. Home Depot's unique article keeps only a153-byte savings
promotion, losing products and disclaimers. Grokipedia's130-byte main is merely
topic suggestions, not article prose. Consumer Reports and Ulta retain more useful
homepage-discovery content but lose outside organizational/service context and
still contain placeholder/carousel noise. These observations support explicit
opt-in, **not treating smaller output as successful content retrieval**. Button
labels can establish nonemptiness; this does not filter login/control shells.

Use ordinary extraction or an explicit alternate scope when context matters.
Captured bodies and document-level source metadata retain their original scope;
focus does not make descriptions, alternates or source tables scope-local.
No new website, interaction/passkey, SDK or challenge acceptance is claimed.
